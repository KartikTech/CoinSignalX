Privacy Policy for CoinSignalX

Effective Date: November 2026

CoinSignalX ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CoinSignalX mobile application (package name: com.coinsignalx.crypto). This page informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our app.

1. Information We Collect

CoinSignalX does not require account registration with your name, email address, phone number, or financial credentials. To securely deliver real-time market updates, the app uses:

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication: Generates a random, non-personal anonymous identifier to authenticate read access to our signal feed.
  • Cloud Firestore & Diagnostics: Basic device and crash diagnostics collected automatically by Google Play Services and Firebase to maintain app stability.

2. Non-Custodial & Financial Disclaimer

CoinSignalX is strictly an informational market analysis and trade-logging application. We do not operate as a cryptocurrency exchange, brokerage, or wallet, and we never collect wallet private keys, exchange API keys, or user funds.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google Firebase services, governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because authentication is anonymous, no personal profile data is stored on our servers. Clearing app data or uninstalling CoinSignalX permanently removes your local anonymous session identifier.
